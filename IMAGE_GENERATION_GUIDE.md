# Corpus — Image Generation & Visual Assets Guide

This document outlines the visual directions, subject matter, artistic themes, and technical specifications for generating imagery for **Onboarding Screens** and **Home Screen Studio Cards** in the **Corpus** app.

---

## 🏛️ Overall Aesthetic Identity

**Corpus** is an editorial art-history decision layer and tattoo design tool. All generated imagery should reflect a luxury, museum-grade aesthetic rather than generic AI stock art or vibrant pop graphics.

* **Core Color Palette**:
  * **Background / Darks**: Ink obsidian (`#16151A`), deep chiaroscuro shadows (`#232229`).
  * **Neutrals / Textures**: Warm bone (`#EDE6D6`), muted parchment (`#C9BFA8`), warm tan (`#C9A987`).
  * **Accent Highlights**: Verdigris / aged copper patina (`#4F6358` / `#6B8A7A`).
* **Lighting & Atmosphere**: Low-key, dramatic chiaroscuro side lighting (single light source, raking lights over carved or textured surfaces). High contrast, crisp silhouettes, and deep dark negative space.
* **Materials & Textures**: Carved marble, dark obsidian stone, heavy archival paper grain, graphite, sumi ink washes, fine-line hatching, whip-shading dotwork, and etched copper.

---

## 1. Onboarding Screen Images

Onboarding images serve as the backdrop for key onboarding steps. They appear in a vertical container with a dark linear gradient at the bottom.

* **Recommended Aspect Ratio**: `9:16` or `3:4` vertical portrait (Minimum resolution: **1080 × 1920 px**).
* **Composition Rule**: Ensure the primary subject sits near the top half or center, leaving dark negative space towards the bottom where text overlays sit.

---

### 📱 Screen 1: Linguistic Precision
* **App Feature**: Phrase & inscription verification against classical grammar, Latin/Greek history, and biblical context.
* **Tagline / Theme**: *"Before it’s permanent, ensure it’s accurate."*
* **What Type of Picture to Generate**:
  * **Subject**: A classical Greco-Roman marble bust or statue (e.g., Marcus Aurelius, Apollo, or a Stoic philosopher) in deep shadow, with crisp Latin or classical inscription lettering finely chiselled onto a nearby dark marble stone block.
  * **Composition**: Dramatic profile or three-quarter angle of the marble sculpture emerging from a dark obsidian background. Heavy raking light highlighting marble grain, chisel marks, and contour lines.
  * **Mood**: Stoic, authoritative, eternal, academic accuracy.
* **Target File Path**: `assets/images/onboarding_precision.jpg` (or `assets/style_samples/detailed_microrealism_statue.jpg`)

---

### 📱 Screen 2: The Vault
* **App Feature**: Art history codex exploring historical movements (Neoclassicism, Irezumi, Baroque, Cyber-sigilism) and motifs.
* **Tagline / Theme**: *"Root your design in true artistic heritage."*
* **What Type of Picture to Generate**:
  * **Subject**: A dramatic split artwork or museum gallery fusion combining classical Roman/Greek marble sculpture on one side, seamlessly blending into Japanese sumi-e ink waves, Renaissance engraving cross-hatching, and medieval manuscript ornaments.
  * **Composition**: Vertical composition with a central focal element (such as a split classical face or historic motif) emerging from a deep dark backdrop. High contrast between marble grain and fine sumi ink strokes.
  * **Mood**: Archival, encyclopedic, timeless, curated authority.
* **Target File Path**: `assets/images/onboarding_vault.jpg` (or `assets/images/vault.jpg`)

---

### 📱 Screen 3: The Concierge
* **App Feature**: Interactive AI design assistant translating abstract thoughts into professional tattoo briefs.
* **Tagline / Theme**: *"Translate your abstract ideas into a professional brief."*
* **What Type of Picture to Generate**:
  * **Subject**: An artist’s atelier study or classical drafting table with open parchment sketchbooks, graphite compasses, and an illustrative line art motif (such as a coiled serpent, winged phoenix, or classical drapery) drawn on fine aged paper.
  * **Composition**: Close-up studio tabletop angle with warm, low spotlighting casting soft shadows over drafting tools and fine-line ink sketches.
  * **Mood**: Creative, intellectual, collaborative, archival.
* **Target File Path**: `assets/images/onboarding_concierge.jpg` (or `assets/style_samples/illustrative_serpent_forearm.jpg`)

## 2. Home Screen Studio Cards

Home screen cards represent the core tools in the Corpus suite. They sit inside matted frames with dark surfaces and subtle borders.

---

### 🎨 Card 1 (Feature Card): AI Concierge Studio
* **Title**: `AI Concierge Studio`
* **Tagline**: `Museum of thought`
* **Role**: The prominent primary studio tile for generating custom design briefs.
* **Recommended Aspect Ratio**: `16:9` or `4:3` landscape (Minimum resolution: **1200 × 900 px**).
* **What Type of Picture to Generate**:
  * **Subject**: A luxury art archive or atelier environment. Heavy oak or obsidian drafting desk with illuminated technical drawings, calipers, fine ink pens, and a glowing, subtle verdigris/gold ethereal linework design hovering over an open sketchbook.
  * **Composition**: Panoramic or wide angle, rich in depth and dark atmosphere.
  * **Mood**: Master workshop, intellectual luxury, synthesis of human heritage and modern AI intelligence.
* **Target File Path**: `assets/images/concierge.jpg`

---

### 🎨 Card 2 (Grid Card): The Vault
* **Title**: `The Vault`
* **Tagline**: `Art history and motifs`
* **Role**: Codex exploring historical art movements (Neoclassicism, Baroque, Irezumi, Bauhaus, Cyber-sigilism, etc.) and motifs.
* **Recommended Aspect Ratio**: `3:4` portrait or `1:1` square (Minimum resolution: **800 × 1000 px**).
* **What Type of Picture to Generate**:
  * **Subject**: A museum archive gallery corridor or a dramatic split artwork combining classical marble sculpture with Japanese sumi-e ink waves, Renaissance engraving cross-hatching, and medieval manuscript ornaments.
  * **Composition**: Centered perspective or dramatic collage, emphasizing historical art movements.
  * **Mood**: Timeless, encyclopedic, curated, historic.
* **Target File Path**: `assets/images/vault.jpg`

---

### 🎨 Card 3 (Grid Card): Verify
* **Title**: `Verify`
* **Tagline**: `Etched in stone`
* **Role**: Translation & grammar validation engine for phrases and inscriptions.
* **Recommended Aspect Ratio**: `3:4` portrait or `1:1` square (Minimum resolution: **800 × 1000 px**).
* **What Type of Picture to Generate**:
  * **Subject**: A dark, weather-worn obsidian or granite slab with sharp, crisp chiselled Roman/Latin typography (e.g., "MEMENTO MORI" or "PER ASPERA AD ASTRA") cut into the stone, illuminated by sharp side light.
  * **Composition**: Macro close-up on the carved stone letters with fine dust and stone texture.
  * **Mood**: Permanent, unyielding, precise, ancient.
* **Target File Path**: `assets/images/verify.jpg`

---

### 🎨 Card 4 (Grid Card): Taste
* **Title**: `Taste`
* **Tagline**: `Your visual DNA`
* **Role**: Personal aesthetic taste profile, style swiping, and artistic alignment.
* **Recommended Aspect Ratio**: `3:4` portrait or `1:1` square (Minimum resolution: **800 × 1000 px**).
* **What Type of Picture to Generate**:
  * **Subject**: An abstract human iris or topographic fingerprint motif constructed purely out of fine tattoo linework—combining stippled dotwork, single-needle contours, and whip-shading gradients.
  * **Composition**: Centered macro shot against dark background, showcasing line weight variations.
  * **Mood**: Identity, precision, personal DNA, artistic fingerprint.
* **Target File Path**: `assets/images/taste.jpg`

---

### 🎨 Card 5 (Grid Card): Preview
* **Title**: `Preview`
* **Tagline**: `See it on skin`
* **Role**: Placement preview tool to simulate tattoo design overlay on human body anatomy.
* **Recommended Aspect Ratio**: `3:4` portrait or `1:1` square (Minimum resolution: **800 × 1000 px**).
* **What Type of Picture to Generate**:
  * **Subject**: An elegant silhouette of human anatomy (such as a shoulder blade, forearm, or calf) with a fine, subtle geometric grid or architectural projection lines lightly tracing the contours of the skin.
  * **Composition**: Low-key lighting, focused on body contours and subtle projection lines.
  * **Mood**: Anatomical precision, tactile skin canvas, modern visualization.
* **Target File Path**: `assets/images/preview.jpg`

## 🛠️ Developer Checklist After Generating Images

1. **Save Images**: Place generated JPG/PNG images into `assets/images/` with the exact filenames listed above.
2. **Verify Code References**:
   - `lib/screens/onboarding_screen.dart` displays 3 core pillars: **Linguistic Precision**, **The Vault**, and **The Concierge**.
   - `lib/screens/home_screen.dart` links **The Vault** tile to `assets/images/vault.jpg`.
3. **Verify `pubspec.yaml`**:
   Ensure `pubspec.yaml` contains:
   ```yaml
   flutter:
     assets:
       - assets/images/
       - assets/style_samples/
   ```
4. **Test**: Run `flutter run` or perform a hot restart to confirm all images render cleanly within the matted frames and onboarding pages.
