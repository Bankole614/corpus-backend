## 📥 Top Recommended Image Sources

### 1. Open-Access Museum Collections (100% Free Public Domain)
* **The Metropolitan Museum of Art (The Met Open Access):** [metmuseum.org/art/collection](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search)
  * *Best for:* Greek marble busts, Renaissance engravings, Ukiyo-e woodblock prints, medieval armor, manuscripts.
* **The Rijksmuseum (Amsterdam):** [rijksmuseum.nl/en/rijksstudio](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/rijksstudio)
  * *Best for:* Dutch Golden Age *Vanitas*, skulls, hourglasses, Rembrandt chiaroscuro.
* **The Art Institute of Chicago:** [artic.edu/collection](https://www.artic.edu/collection)
  * *Best for:* Impressionism, Post-Impressionism, Art Nouveau illustrations, Neoclassical statuary.
* **British Museum Collection:** [britishmuseum.org/collection](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection)
  * *Best for:* Egyptian amulets, Mesoamerican stelae, Asian devotional art, classical coins & artifacts.

### 2. Free High-Resolution Stock Platforms
* **Unsplash:** [unsplash.com](https://unsplash.com)
  * *Search terms:* `"marble bust statue dark background"`, `"vintage brass compass"`, `"dark baroque painting"`, `"skull memento mori"`.
* **Pexels:** [pexels.com](https://www.pexels.com)
* **Pixabay:** [pixabay.com](https://pixabay.com)

### 3. AI Image Generation (Midjourney / DALL-E 3 / Leonardo.ai)
* **Prompt Template:**
  > `"Black and grey fine-line microrealism tattoo design of [SUBJECT], studio lighting, obsidian dark background, 8k resolution, photorealistic"`

## 🛠️ How to Code Your Image URLs in `lib/data/codex_data.dart`

When you have uploaded your images to your CDN or storage bucket (e.g. AWS S3, Cloudinary, Supabase Storage, or `vaultBaseUrl`), open `lib/data/codex_data.dart` and paste your image URLs into the corresponding object:

```dart
CodexMovement(
  id: 'neoclassicism',
  title: 'Neoclassicism & Statuary',
  era: '18th – 19th Century',
  subtitle: 'Marble forms, Stoic order, and ideal human anatomy',
  ...
  imageUrl: '$vaultBaseUrl/neoclassicism_1.jpg', // Slide 1 (Main Card Image)
  additionalImages: [
    '$vaultBaseUrl/neoclassicism_2.jpg', // Slide 2 (Detail)
    '$vaultBaseUrl/neoclassicism_3.jpg', // Slide 3 (Tattoo Example)
  ],
  category: 'CLASSICAL',
),
```
